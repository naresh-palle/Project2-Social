Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with the Meta Platform Terms and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users may request the deletion of their personal data associated with CR8 Studio at any time.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can request data deletion using either of the following methods:

Method 1: Inside the App (Automated)

  1. Log in to your CR8 Studio account.
  2. Navigate to Settings from the dashboard.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Type "DELETE" and click Delete Account.

Note: This will immediately queue your account, analytics data, and social media authorizations for permanent deletion.

Method 2: Removing Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Access

If you only want to remove CR8 Studio's access to your Facebook or Instagram data:

  1. Go to your Facebook account's Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps and Websites.
  3. Find "CR8 Studio" and click Remove.

Method 3: Email Request

You may also request complete data deletion by emailing us directly. Please send an email to privacy@cr8.studio with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Include the email address associated with your account.
