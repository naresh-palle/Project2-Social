Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. Introduction

flugr ("we", "us") is the data controller for personal data processed through our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your data.

2. Data We Collect

We collect account data (name, email, phone, profile), usage data (posts, messages, interactions), device data (IP, browser, session identifiers), payment metadata (processed by PCI-compliant partners), and optional analytics with your consent.

3. Third-Party Social Media Integrations & API Usage

When you connect your social media accounts (e.g., YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X), we access your data via their official APIs to display your analytics and metrics on your profile. We do not store your passwords. Your data is not sold to third parties.

  • Google & YouTube: By connecting your YouTube account, you agree to be bound by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. You may revoke our access to your data at any time via the Google Security Settings page.
  • Meta (Instagram & Facebook): We process your data in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms. You may manage or revoke permissions via your Instagram and Facebook app settings.
  • X (Twitter): We access basic read-only data for analytics. You may revoke this access via the connected apps page in your X account settings.

4. How We Use Data

To operate accounts, facilitate collaborations, process payments, send service notifications, improve the Platform, comply with law, and send marketing communications (with consent).

5. Sharing

We share data with payment processors, cloud hosting providers, email vendors, and analytics tools under strict data processing agreements. We do not sell personal data.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing. EU/UK users may lodge complaints with their supervisory authority. Submit requests via privacy@flugr.app.

7. Children

The Platform is not directed at children under 16. We delete accounts of underage users upon discovery.
